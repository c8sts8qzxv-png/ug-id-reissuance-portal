How a replacement is processed

Five stages, and you can see which one you are in.

The stages have not changed. What changed is that each one is recorded against your reference number, timed, and sent to you as it happens.

  1. 01

    Request submitted

    You state the reason and upload the police report or affidavit.

    You
  2. 02

    Payment verified

    The GH₵ 50 reissuance fee is matched against your reference.

    Payment platform
  3. 03

    Officer review

    Your record is checked and the request is approved or returned with a reason.

    ID Card Unit
  4. 04

    In the print batch

    Approved requests are queued and the card is printed with its serial.

    ID Card Unit
  5. 05

    Ready for collection

    You are told the day it is ready and you confirm receipt at the counter.

    You

The counter process

A form in a tray

  • — A paper form is filed with no reference number attached to it.
  • — Your record is checked by hand against a paper register.
  • — Nothing is sent to you, at any stage.
  • — The only way to check is to walk to the office and ask.
  • — A misfiled form means starting the whole request again.
  • — Nobody can say how long a replacement takes on average.

This portal

A request you can follow

  • ✓ A reference number is issued the moment you submit.
  • ✓ Your record and your payment are matched automatically.
  • ✓ An SMS and e-mail go out at every change of stage.
  • ✓ The current stage is on your screen, with the date it was reached.
  • ✓ Nothing can be misfiled — the request is the record.
  • ✓ Turnaround and backlog are reported to the Registrar's office.

Before you start

What you need to have ready.

Document

Police report or affidavit

Required for a lost or stolen card. For a damaged card, bring the old card to the counter when you collect the new one. PDF, PNG or JPG up to 5 MB.

Fee

GH₵ 50.00

Paid by mobile money or bank card

Paid through the University's existing payment platform. Keep the receipt reference.

Collection

ID Card Unit, Legon

Registrar's Office Annex, Monday to Friday, 09:00–16:00. Bring one other form of identification and your reference number.
